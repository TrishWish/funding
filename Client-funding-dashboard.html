    How utilisation is calculated

    Utilisation is calculated the same way for the plan overall and for each support category, using that category's own Released, Spent and Pending.

    Committed
    Spent to date + Pending
    Expected
    Released × (time elapsed ÷ period length)
    Gap
    Committed − Expected
    Under-utilised
    Spending less than the plan's pace suggests
    Over-utilised
    Spending more than the plan's pace suggests
    On track
    Within 10% of the released amount either way

    Period end is the next scheduled release date when known, otherwise the plan end date (shown as approximate).
